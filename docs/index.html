Awalé is the classic West African seed-sowing strategy game of the oware / mancala family, rebuilt for the browser. Play a four-level AI, a friend on the same device, or anyone online. Works offline and installs like an app.

The rules in four lines

Two rows of six pits, four seeds in each. You own the row nearest you.

  1. SowTake every seed from one of your pits and drop them one by one, counterclockwise.
  2. CaptureIf your last seed makes an opponent's pit hold 2 or 3, take them, along with the pits just before it that also hold 2 or 3.
  3. FeedIf your opponent has no seeds left, you must play a move that gives them some. You can't take all their seeds in one move.
  4. WinThe first player to capture 25 seeds wins.

Under the board

React and TypeScript on the front end, one Cloudflare Worker behind it. The rules are shared by the browser, the server and the tests, so they can't disagree.

Front end
React, TypeScript, Vite, PWA service worker
Engine
Pure TypeScript engine and AI, no DOM, a port of a long-standing C implementation
Server
Cloudflare Worker with Durable Objects for rooms, lobby, identity, leaderboard and stats
Mobile
Capacitor shells for iOS and Android
Tests
Unit and self-play suites, Playwright end-to-end tests, verified puzzles, run in CI on every push
